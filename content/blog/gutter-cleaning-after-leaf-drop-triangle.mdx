Gutter cleaning in Raleigh NC has a late-fall timing that a spring visit does not cover. Maples and sweetgums often thin out first. Many of the oaks do not. Willow oaks, which line a lot of streets and yards in Raleigh, Cary, and Apex, commonly hold their leaves longer and drop a heavy share in late November and into early December. Some years a windy week does most of it at once. Clean the week the maples finish, and the trough can be full again after the oaks let go.

A gutter packed with those wet leaves does not move water the way an open one does. In a long winter rain, water can spill back over the outside edge instead of reaching the downspout. That overflow can streak siding and keep fascia, mulch beds, and the ground along the foundation wet. [Gutter cleaning](/gutter-cleaning) means clearing the leaves and debris and flushing the downspouts so the run is moving water again. If we find a blockage or damage the flush does not clear, we will tell you.

This is a different visit from a spring or mid-year cleaning. Spring debris in the Triangle is pollen, seeds, and whatever the trees shed after they flower. Late fall is a mat of whole leaves sitting in the trough ahead of colder rain. The year-round reason to clean before water is already pouring over the edge is in [why gutter cleaning matters more than most homeowners think](/blog/why-gutter-cleaning-matters-more-than-most-homeowners-think). That post is the general case. This one is only the window after leaf drop.

From the eaves, black streaks on the shingles are easy to spot and easy to misread. On asphalt roofs in this humidity, those streaks are algae growth, often the kind called Gloeocapsa magma, not just the shingles looking older. Taking them off is a [roof washing](/roof-washing) job, done as a [soft wash](/soft-washing), and it is quoted on its own.

Homes under a heavy oak canopy often need this fall cleaning even when the gutters were cleaned earlier in the year. Plan on a cleaning after pollen and another after the leaves, and more often where the tree cover is thick.
