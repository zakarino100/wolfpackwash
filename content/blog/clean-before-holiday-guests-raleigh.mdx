Holiday guests in Raleigh, Cary, and Apex decide how the house feels before anyone opens the door. They see the front elevation, the glass, and the walk from the car. By late November the yellow pollen is long gone. What is often still there is the film this climate leaves on a shaded wall: algae and mildew, especially on north-facing siding and under trees, plus dull glass and a dark driveway or walk. That is the view you are actually hosting.

House washing before Christmas is a [house wash](/house-washing): a soft wash, low pressure and a cleaning solution on the siding. That is how algae, mold, and mildew come off vinyl, brick, stucco, and wood, which is the staining guests notice on a Triangle front this time of year. The wash is the walls. Windows, the driveway, and the walk are named on the quote.

The glass is the other thing people look at on the way in. [Window cleaning](/window-cleaning) pairs with a house wash, and the two are often done on the same visit. If you want the panes, frames, and sills to match the siding, say so when you request the quote so the windows are listed there.

The driveway and the front walk are quoted on their own, and they can still be done on that same visit.
